# Fresh Life 🌿

A calm, single-page daily-routine dashboard for a researcher & global-market trader
living between **China** and **Sweden**.

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the live site (GitHub Pages) once enabled.

**Features**
- Dual live China/Sweden clocks with a switchable region schedule
- Habit tracker with streaks and gentle science-backed nudges
- 15-minute guided timers (yoga, tai chi, forest run, facial care, focus 50/10, eye rest)
- Live US / HK / China A-share market hours in both your local times
- Rotating weight-loss diet blending Chinese & Nordic kitchens
- **Interruption rebalance** plans (missed sleep, late/heavy meal, late US session, and more)
- China↔Sweden jet-lag reset plans
- Light/dark themes; all progress saved privately in your browser

General wellness guidance grounded in circadian, nutrition & exercise research —
not personalized medical advice.
